Raw idea: “Students use AI all the time. I want a short carousel about using it well without blindly trusting it.”

- Audience: students and early-career professionals
- Platforms: Instagram and LinkedIn
- Goal: give a practical, memorable routine
- Tone: encouraging, direct, not alarmist
- CTA: try one small workflow this week
- Format: six slides, 1080 × 1350, one idea per slide
- Guardrails: no invented statistics, no unsupported performance claims, and no suggestion that AI output is automatically reliable
